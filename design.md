# VisionTrack design
White-first precision workshop SaaS. Neon violet #801CFF used as solid accent, no purple-on-white gradients. Deep ink #181322, warm off-white #F8F8FA, hairline borders #EAE8EF, muted lilac #F2EAFF. Emerald for secure/ready states. Small purple neon glow only on recording CTA.
Typography: Poppins headings and body, 13px controls, 14px body, 30px section titles, 40px numeric metrics. Explicit letterspacing on small uppercase labels.
Desktop: 232px white sidebar with logomark, grouped navigation and a muted plan card at bottom; content with 38px inset, breadcrumb header, greeting, 4 compact bordered metric columns, 65/35 recording split, recent-video table. Main viewport generous whitespace, thin separators. Responsive: sidebar becomes compact top navigation, grids stack at 900px, forms and table remain usable on mobile.
Webcam card dark ink with illustrative workshop photograph while disconnected, clearly tagged as illustration, live video only after explicit permission. White camera affordance; recording state red dot, elapsed timer and silent badge. Right panel contains customer/device/service fields, consent confirmation and one vivid purple primary action. All real empty states; no fabricated recordings or activity.
Shared components: 12px corner radius, white panels, minimal shadow; button height 44px, disabled/loading/error feedback. Icons: Lucide 18px. Auth modal clean white, dual tabs, owner Google verification. Client viewer contains only video and repair metadata, never sidebar/admin data. Private assets with presigned reads.
Motion: 300ms entry fade, restrained hover/active shifts; reduced-motion support. Focus visible purple ring. Brand mark: violet outlined focus/viewfinder shape with inner vertical pill; wordmark visiontrack, small uppercase WORKSPACE subtitle.
No Runable badge. Preserve analytics and development feedback.
